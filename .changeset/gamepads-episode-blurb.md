Game controllers, such as a Bluetooth PS5 DualSense, now navigate Luma like the remote: D-pad
or left stick to move (repeating while held), Cross/A to select, Circle/B for Back, Triangle/Y or
Options for options, Square/X for the trailer and the PS button for the top of Home. A title
page's focused episode now shows its synopsis under the episode row, hidden until watched when
spoilers are hidden.
